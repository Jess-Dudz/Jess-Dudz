## LakeLifeIQ

**[lakelifeiq.com](https://lakelifeiq.com)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lakelifeiq-platform-concept) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `TypeScript` `Next.js` `Postgres`

A live decision-support platform for recreational boat buyers. Most boat advice
stops at the boat. This maps the whole setup around it: dock compatibility, lift
and cover requirements, comfort upgrades, and which local providers can actually
deliver them inside a real budget.

<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lakelifeiq-platform-concept/raw/main/public/screenshots/launch-screeenshots/lakelife-hero.png" width="49%" alt="LakeLifeIQ landing page"/>
  <img src="https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lakelifeiq-platform-concept/raw/main/public/screenshots/launch-screeenshots/lakelife-setup-results.png" width="49%" alt="Personalized results"/>
</p>
<p align="center">
  <img src="https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lakelifeiq-platform-concept/raw/main/public/screenshots/launch-screeenshots/lakelife-setup-flow.png" width="49%" alt="Guided setup flow"/>
  <img src="https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lakelifeiq-platform-concept/raw/main/public/screenshots/launch-screeenshots/lakelife-dealer.png" width="49%" alt="Dealer directory"/>
</p>

- Rules-based recommendation engine scored on usage, dock type, budget, and stated priorities
- Provider ranking uses Bayesian shrinkage, so a business with 3 reviews can't outrank one with 300
- Full click attribution: a plan creates a session that follows the user through to the dealer click and the lead
- Every database read falls back to a static catalogue, so the site degrades instead of erroring

## Tornado Risk Intelligence

**[live app](https://spatiotemporal-tornado-trends-etl-33lzqces6fp8zwndje4ch6.streamlit.app/)** &nbsp;·&nbsp; [source](https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/spatiotemporal-tornado-trends-etl) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Python` `scikit-learn` `Streamlit`

Historical storm data turned into an interactive risk scoring system. The

has been shifting later in the day over the last seventy years.

## Lake Region Market Priority Analysis

[source](https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lake-market-analysis) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `R` `Census ACS`

A weighted scoring index built to allocate a real marketing budget across
geographic regions, using Census income and property value data rather than
population alone. Built for a regional service provider, but the method
generalizes to any premium home or outdoor service.

## Lake of the Ozarks Property Intelligence

[source](https://github.com/Jess-Dudz/lake-ozarks-property-intelligence) &nbsp;·&nbsp; `Excel` `scoring model`

Active listings and recent sales combined into one dataset, then scored and

## Background

Completing an M.S. in Data Science, with an MBA in marketing and an MA in
English alongside it. Before analytics I worked in operations leadership,
marketing, and e-commerce, which is where the habit of asking what a number is
actually *for* comes from.

Most people doing segmentation arrived from statistics and picked up the
marketing later. I came the other way, which is why "does this change what
anyone does" tends to be my first question rather than my last.

The English degree is more useful than it sounds. Analysis rarely fails at the
math. It fails at the explanation.
